Where the price changes

The price rises by Rs 7,300 overall (730% above the mine-gate price), with two modeled drops. The largest increase is Rs 1,000 at stop 23. Each change is the difference between consecutive quotes and may include costs as well as margin.

How to verify the route

Match the same stone across receipts using its weight, grade, photographs and transaction dates. Record actual payments separately from asking prices. The mine-gate share in this scenario is Rs 1,000 ÷ Rs 8,300 × 100 = 12.05%.
